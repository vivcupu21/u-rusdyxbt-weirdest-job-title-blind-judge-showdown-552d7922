## The entry

> **Senior Tooth Fairy Claims Adjuster** (34 characters)

A real-sounding corporate title — Senior Claims Adjuster — with one
hilariously specific twist: the claims are for teeth left under pillows.
It reads instantly, pictures a person at a desk stamping claim forms for
lost teeth, and lands on the first read.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the stored title from `entry.txt`
byte-identically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, the artifact the
competition gate reads. No network, no fonts, no third-party packages.

## Run locally

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
